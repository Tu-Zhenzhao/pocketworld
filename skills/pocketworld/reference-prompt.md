Do not attach frames from someone else's loops as references. That copies their picture. Describe the subject yourself.

## Worked example — rain at the bus stop

Square 1:1 illustration of a tiny isometric diorama floating on a pure white background, lots of empty white margin. One small scene only, centered, occupying roughly the middle 70% of the width and half the height. Nothing else in the frame.

Camera: 2:1 dimetric. Horizontal edges run 2 pixels across for every 1 pixel down. Vertical edges (people, posts, walls) are straight up and down, not leaned back. No perspective, no vanishing point, no front view.

Look: chunky low-resolution pixels, about a 360 pixel grid scaled up with nearest-neighbor. Flat colors, around 14 colors total plus white. Ordered dither where the ground fades into the white. Soft light from the upper left, simple three-tone shading, hard edges. No anti-aliasing, no bloom, no glow, no outline, no film grain, no CRT, no text, no watermark.

Subject: A quiet country bus stop in the rain at dusk. A young man sits alone on the bench inside a simple shelter, head slightly bowed. A few steps away, a young woman in a mustard raincoat holds a dusty-rose umbrella and looks at him.

Setting: The shelter and its bench, one warm street lamp, a strip of wet road, a grass verge that dithers away to white. No bicycle, no child, no extra props.

Palette: Muted dusky blue-grey and teal for the rain, the road, and the shelter. Warm amber from the lamp pooling on the wet asphalt. The rose umbrella and the mustard coat are the only other warm colors. Reflections of the lamp and both figures sit in the road.

Mood: tender, quiet, cinematic.

People: Exactly two. The man is seated, small, head lowered toward the woman. The woman stands in the rain, body turned a little toward him so her gaze reads across the gap.

Motion, frozen in this still: Diagonal rain, rings in a puddle, a drop leaving the umbrella rim. The people are still.

Avoid: photorealism, smooth 3D render, full-bleed composition, cropped edges, multiple panels, isometric 30-degree with equal x/y, anime line art, depth of field, lens flare.
