# Drawing recipes by subject

Read only relevant categories. Observe suitable visual references at the chosen view, then simplify to meaningful forms at the actual cell budget. These procedures are not a universal rig or guaranteed anatomy.

| Subject | Decide first | Pixel refinement | Return to structure when… |
|---|---|---|---|
| People | line of action, head/torso/pelvis, joints, supports, negative spaces | face direction, purposeful hand/grip, a few cloth planes | posture needs explanation; limbs stretch; joints dislocate; face is random noise |
| Quadrupeds | species skull/muzzle, neck/shoulder/ribs/pelvis, joint bends, horn/ear roots, weight | species silhouette; near/far limb overlap; hoof/paw and contact | horns alone distinguish species; reptile-like head; arbitrary tucked lumps; floating body |
| Props/buildings | construction planes, thickness, projection axes, supports, relative scale | connected cell-wide slats/rims/seams/tubes; restrained highlights | hoop becomes fuzzy disk; legs miss ground; frame thickness changes randomly |
| Plants | clusters, roots, trunk taper/branches, quiet zones | varied leaf planes/heights; sparse hero blades; perimeter fade | uniform checker/speckle; identical balls as crown; foliage hides the action |
| Water | plane, depth, contact/event sources, current | broad quiet colors, localized arcs/glints, source-linked reflection | surface boils; rings lack contact; reflection ignores its source |
| Fabric | anchors, gravity, volume, main folds |2–3 planes, overlapping motion, contact folds | sticker-like sliding texture; uniform sway; revealed painted holes |
| Light/effects | emitter/caster and affected/occluded surfaces | stable pools, material-specific highlights, sparse particles | glow crosses blockers; whole frame flickers; effects hide poor drawing |

## People

Block line of action and masses before color. Check projected shoulders/pelvis/elbows/knees/contact points with a suited reference for complex poses. Keep limb lengths; use constraints/IK for interactions. Sleeping posture comes from supported shoulders, pelvis and limbs, not merely closed eyes.

Draw contour and negative spaces before interior marks. Reduce fingers to a clear grasp or relaxed hand, retaining the attachment. Align face accents to its plane; a hat-covered face needs no invented eyes. Design clothing separately from skin and let it follow connected anatomy.

Breathing holds support contacts and deforms connected chest/abdomen cross-sections. Keep transitions at waist/upper chest continuous; let touching hands/garments respond where appropriate. A translating hard-colored belly patch, or head/hat bobbing with it, is insufficient. Inspect inhale/rest/exhale and consecutive frames; repair shape, not only amplitude.

## Animals

Choose 3–5 species-specific silhouette cues from a useful reference. For a resting buffalo: broad low ribs, shoulder/neck transition, skull/muzzle proportions, lateral ears/swept horn roots, folded-joint layout and supported underside. Do not transfer one guessed buffalo rig to horses/dogs/cats.

Map near/far limbs even when hidden. Check floor and rider supports. Chewing uses hinge/side-grind around a stable skull, coherent cheek response and mouth-held vegetation from the same anchor. Avoid translating the whole head or a closed-eye dash when awake. Tiny teeth/tongue rarely fix an unreadable jaw.

## Hard surfaces

Model planes/thickness first. Decide which thin structures deserve a connected cell. Preserve a basket rim's open hole rather than averaging a disk; connect pole, base and backboard. Structural dark marks are allowed. Readability may intentionally exaggerate physical thickness; acknowledge the stylization.

## Plants, water and atmosphere

Grass uses a few tuft families with fixed roots, varied heights/facings and common wind with spatial phase. Keep mouths/faces/hands clear. Mouth grass follows the jaw; rooted grass follows wind. Trees need taper/branching silhouette before uneven crown masses, planes/gaps and last-pass surface detail.

Keep water quiet between events. Derive disturbance origin/start from contact; transform the same source for simplified reflection, then distort through the surface. A coherent approximation is acceptable if described honestly. Keep glow and particles subordinate to actors and geometry.

## Review evidence

For each hero: native silhouette, final-size still, integer crop, action extremes, consecutive frames and checked supports/attachments. Unclear animal identity or coarse breathing remains an art defect even if export and numeric checks pass.
